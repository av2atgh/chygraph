\chapter*{Notation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Notation}
\markboth{Notation}{Notation}

Symbols carried over from the papers this book draws on are kept as they are
there, so that a reader moving between book and paper does not have to
retranslate. The cost is that a few letters do more than one job. Every such
letter is listed below with the chapters in which each meaning is in force. Two
of them carry two meanings within one chapter --- $m$ in
Chapter~\ref{ch:statmech} and $\eta$ in Chapter~\ref{ch:colouring}. In both
cases the second meaning is the standard symbol in the literature being matched,
and renaming it would cost the reader more than the clash does; the text says so
at the point where the second meaning enters.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{The structure.}

\begin{longtable}{@{}l@{\hspace{1.2em}}L{2.55in}@{}}
$\ave{\,\cdot\,}$ & average over the ensemble\\[2pt]
atom & a vertex that contains nothing\\[2pt]
complex & a vertex that contains other vertices\\[2pt]
layer, $l$, $m$ & a set of complexes of one kind; $L$ is their number\\[2pt]
$c$ & cardinality: how many vertices a complex contains\\[2pt]
$\kappa$ & chy-degree: how many complexes contain a given vertex\\[2pt]
$\kbar$ & excess chy-degree, $\ave{\kappa(\kappa-1)}/\ave{\kappa}$\\[2pt]
$k$, $\bar k$ & degree and excess degree of a node of a \emph{graph}\\[2pt]
$s$, $\sbar$ & intra-complex component size, and its excess\\[2pt]
$\Phi$, $\bar\Phi$ & chy-degree generating function, and its inclusion-biased
                     (excess) form\\[2pt]
$G$, $\bar G$ & intra-complex generating functions\\
\end{longtable}

\noindent\textbf{Letters that do more than one job.}

\begin{longtable}{@{}l@{\hspace{1.2em}}L{2.55in}@{}}
$q$ & bond or hyperedge occupation probability (Part~II); number of Potts
      states (Ch.~\ref{ch:potts}) or colours (Ch.~\ref{ch:colouring}); number
      of members of the unanimity complex (Sec.~\ref{sec:unanimity})\\[3pt]
$p$ & site occupation probability (Part~II), and the within-household
      transmission probability $p_{H}$ of Ch.~\ref{ch:epidemics}; bond
      occupation probability (Part~III)\\[3pt]
$\tau$ & transmission factor $\tau_{c}(q,v)$ (Chs.~\ref{ch:potts},
         \ref{ch:statmech}, \ref{ch:colouring}); degree exponent of a
         hyperbolic random graph (Chs.~\ref{ch:data}, \ref{ch:cover},
         \ref{ch:overlap}); infectious period (Ch.~\ref{ch:epidemics});
         hard-field message (Chs.~\ref{ch:hittingset},
         \ref{ch:cover})\\[3pt]
$\theta$ & the threshold determinant $-\det(\mathrm{vec}^{2}A)$
           (Chs.~\ref{ch:percolation}, \ref{ch:giant}); a growth or core
           exponent (Chs.~\ref{ch:data}, \ref{ch:cover})\\[3pt]
$\Lambda$, $\lambda$ & distance above threshold, and the Perron root of the
                       Jacobian, $\Lambda=\lambda-1$ (Chs.~\ref{ch:percolation},
                       \ref{ch:giant}); $\lambda$ is also the rate at which
                       warnings arrive at a variable, Eq.~\eqref{eq:nestedwp}
                       (Ch.~\ref{ch:satisfiability})\\[3pt]
$\beta$ & inverse temperature (Part~III); order parameter exponent
          (Ch.~\ref{ch:giant}); infection rate and the contagion couplings
          $\beta_{1},\beta_{2}$ (Ch.~\ref{ch:epidemics})\\[3pt]
$B$ & critical amplitude (Ch.~\ref{ch:giant}); external field
      (Chs.~\ref{ch:introduction}, \ref{ch:ising}); branching matrix
      (Ch.~\ref{ch:statmech}, where it is a matrix and set upright in
      $\det(I-B)$)\\[3pt]
$C$, $c$ & cardinality (throughout); clustering coefficient
           (Chs.~\ref{ch:introduction}, \ref{ch:data}); the amplitude
           denominator $C$ (Ch.~\ref{ch:giant}); the residual $C_{q}$
           (Sec.~\ref{sec:unanimity}); a counting number $c_{R}$
           (Ch.~\ref{ch:overlap}); a clause $C_{a}$
           (Ch.~\ref{ch:satisfiability}); the connectedness probability
           $C_{j}(p)$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:Kn} (Ch.~\ref{ch:epidemics}); the
           colouring thresholds $c_{d}$, $c_{q}$
           (Ch.~\ref{ch:colouring})\\[3pt]
$\delta$ & Kronecker delta (Chs.~\ref{ch:percolation}, \ref{ch:statmech});
           probability that a vertex is deleted (Ch.~\ref{ch:cover})\\[3pt]
$T$ & transmissibility (Ch.~\ref{ch:epidemics}); temperature (Part~III)\\[3pt]
$\mu$ & chemical potential conjugate to the cover size
        (Chs.~\ref{ch:hittingset}, \ref{ch:cover}); an external field, the
        shift $\delta_{\mu}$ of Eq.~\eqref{eq:ensemblemap}
        (Ch.~\ref{ch:statmech}); the mean number of further household
        infections $\mu_{H}$ (Ch.~\ref{ch:epidemics}); the weight that a
        layer-2 clause forces its sub-clause false
        (Ch.~\ref{ch:satisfiability})\\[3pt]
$\rho$ & density of the covering set (Chs.~\ref{ch:hittingset},
         \ref{ch:cover}); the infected fraction
         (Ch.~\ref{ch:epidemics}); the ratio $\rho_{a\to i}$ a complex emits,
         Eq.~\eqref{eq:taudef} (Ch.~\ref{ch:potts}); the Perron root of the
         Jacobian at the fixed point the map reaches, Eq.~\eqref{eq:exchange}
         (Ch.~\ref{ch:statmech})\\[3pt]
$\sigma$ & an Ising spin (Ch.~\ref{ch:ising}); a Potts state or colour
           (Chs.~\ref{ch:potts}, \ref{ch:colouring},
           \ref{ch:satisfiability}); the intermediate hard-field quantity of
           Eq.~\eqref{eq:hs} (Ch.~\ref{ch:hittingset})\\[3pt]
$m$ & a layer index (Chs.~\ref{ch:percolation}, \ref{ch:giant},
      \ref{ch:statmech}); the Parisi
      parameter (Sec.~\ref{sec:onestep} and Chs.~\ref{ch:colouring},
      \ref{ch:satisfiability}, \ref{ch:outlook}, none of which carries a layer
      index); magnetisation (Ch.~\ref{ch:ising})\\[3pt]
$\eta$ & the replica-symmetric cavity message, a distribution over colours
         (Secs.~\ref{sec:colouringdef}--\ref{sec:colouring-scalar}); a warning,
         and then a survey (Ch.~\ref{ch:satisfiability}); the rescaled survey
         $e/q$ of \citet{krzakala2004} (Sec.~\ref{sec:colouring-cq}
         only, where their equations are matched term for term)\\[3pt]
$e$ & the probability that a node is forced, the scalar a colouring survey
      reduces to (Ch.~\ref{ch:colouring}); Euler's number elsewhere, and in
      particular the vertex-cover threshold $\ave{k}=e$
      (Chs.~\ref{ch:introduction}, \ref{ch:hittingset},
      \ref{ch:cover})\\
\end{longtable}
